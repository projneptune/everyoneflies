How to give
What your contribution does

Your gift lifts a child into the air.

₹5,000

covers one young flyer's whole day: their time in the tunnel with an instructor, their snacks, their place at the talks, their transport and doctor's check-up, and the chance to meet people who have done the impossible.

₹50,000

sponsors a batch of ten flyers.

₹5,00,000

is our goal, and it will let around 100 young people fly.

Gifts of any size help.

Funds are received and managed by our partner, Operation Blue Freedom Foundation, which works in adventure sports for people with disabilities. All the money raised goes to running Everyone Flies, and after the event we will share how the funds were spent, along with photos and stories from the day.
